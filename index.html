Horizon 2 Readiness Gates
All seven criteria must be met before the Board votes to trigger Horizon 2 (Oct 2028). Updated quarterly alongside KPIs.
Leadership & Governance
Not Started
ED/CEO filled and operating 12+ months; no single-person dependency on decisions or external relationships; governance ToR in operation.
Financial Resilience
Not Started
Unrestricted reserves at or above Board floor; no single funder exceeds concentration limit; pilot expansion funding ring-fenced from core programme.
Kenya Institutional Architecture
Not Started
Legal and institutional pathway for Kenya operations documented and agreed with EAWLS; no ambiguity in employment, assets or financial authority.
Field Workforce & Duty of Care
Not Started
No open HIGH or CRITICAL duty-of-care findings; legal employer clearly established; career pathway operating for at least one annual cycle.
Impact & Evidence
Not Started
Core Kenya programme meeting agreed outcome indicators on this Board dashboard; management decisions documented as evidence-informed; MEL system operational.
Partnerships & Community
Not Started
Formal agreements in place with KWS, KFS and EAWLS; community consultation and feedback mechanisms operational; no partnership flagged as risk or constraint.
Communications & Brand
Not Started
Communications capacity embedded in team and not founder-dependent; brand architecture consistent across all materials; field content pipeline independent.
